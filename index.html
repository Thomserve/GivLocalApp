Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Full visibility and control of your GivEnergy system - right from your pocket, without relying on any third-party service.

⚡

Real-Time Power Flow

An animated live dashboard shows exactly how power is moving between your solar panels, battery, home, and the grid - updated every few seconds.

📊

Energy Statistics

Track today's solar generation, home consumption, grid import and export at a glance. Know where your energy is coming from and going to.

🔋

Battery Management

One-tap Charge Now, Discharge Now, or Pause. Full battery detail including SOC%, capacity, charge rate, and estimated time to empty or full.

📅

Charge & Discharge Schedules

Create time-window slots for charging and discharging with target SOC levels. Up to 10 charge and discharge slots per device, fully configurable.

🔧

Full Inverter Control

Adjust active power rate, charge and discharge power limits via intuitive sliders. Access live telemetry, write logs, commissioning, and advanced settings.

🔒

Local & Private

Communicates directly with your inverter over your home network via Modbus TCP. No account needed, no data ever leaves your house.

🌍

Remote Access

Access your system from anywhere using Tailscale for a secure zero-config VPN, or configure traditional port forwarding to suit your setup.

📋

Logging & Diagnostics

View write logs and live telemetry direct from the inverter. Built-in diagnostics help identify connection issues and track register-level activity.

🔧

Installer Mode

A dedicated installer section provides access to advanced commissioning and service settings, protected separately from everyday user controls.

How It Works

Up and running in seconds.

1

Connect to your home network

Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as your GivEnergy inverter or gateway.

2

Discover your devices

GivLocal automatically scans your network and finds any compatible GivEnergy devices. Or enter an IP address manually.

3

Monitor and control

View live power flow, check energy stats, manage charge schedules, and adjust inverter settings - all from one app.

4

Optional: Remote Access

Configure remote access to reach your system when you're away from home, on your own terms.

Supported Devices

Works with your GivEnergy kit.

GivLocal supports a wide range of GivEnergy hardware, discovered automatically over your local network.

🌐

Gateway

Connect to your GivEnergy gateway to manage multiple batteries from a single view.

🔋

All In One (AIO)

Full support for AIO units - per-unit stats, battery cell detail, charge & discharge history.

⚡

Hybrid Inverters

Monitor and control GEN1, GEN2, and GEN3 hybrid inverters with full schedule and settings access.

🔌

HV Battery

High-voltage battery systems with full SOC monitoring, cell detail, and charge/discharge control.

🔄

AC-Coupled

Full support for AC-coupled inverter configurations alongside your existing solar setup.

🏠

EMS

Energy Management System devices with complete monitoring and control of your whole-home energy setup.

🚗

EV Charger

Connect via Modbus TCP for direct local control, or via OCPP through Home Assistant or a local OCPP server.
